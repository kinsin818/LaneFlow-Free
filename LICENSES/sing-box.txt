- LaneFlow Free itself is released under the MIT license; the two licenses do not conflict.

- Packaging note: the official sing-box v1.14.3 windows-amd64 zip contains sing-box.exe,
  libcronet.dll, and a LICENSE file. This distribution ships sing-box.exe only. libcronet.dll
  is optional at runtime: it is loaded only for transports that require Cronet, none of which
  the free edition exposes. If you build your own configuration that needs Cronet, download the
  official package and place libcronet.dll next to core/sing-box.exe yourself.
